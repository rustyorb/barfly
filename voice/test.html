Barfly's voice

A test bench for the voice server. This is Chatterbox-Turbo: it clones the voice from a clip in the voices folder. It performs [cough], [chuckle] and [laugh]. It does not hiccup, and it ignores the exaggeration setting the original Chatterbox has.

The bar turns this down as he gets drunk. Slower is also lower, like a tape running down.

Higher is livelier and less predictable; lower is flatter and steadier.
